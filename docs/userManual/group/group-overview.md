# Groups

Group is for binding many projects together. You can see many projects of a group at a glance.
If you're registered as a group member, you also get access authorization for the group's
projects.

Creating Groups
----------------

1. Click the `Plus` button at the top of the page.
1. Click `New Group` on the popup.
1. Fill out the form.
1. Click the `Create Group` button.

Once you create a group, it'll be listed under the `Groups` tab on yona's main page.

You can also see your groups in the popup when you click your avatar at the top of the page.

Setting groups
---------------

You can change your group's name, description, and avatar through `Group Setting`.

1. Go to the group page.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Group Setting` button.
1. Click the `Setting` tab.
1. Change the avatar, name, and description.

Managing members
------------------

You can add and remove users from your group and change a member's authorization.

### Adding members

1. Go to the group page.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Group Setting` button.
1. Click the `Group Member` tab.
1. Type a username or id you want to add.
1. Click the `Add` button.

### Deleting members

1. Go to the group page.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Group Setting` button.
1. Click the `Group Member` tab.
1. Click the `Delete` button to the right of the member you want to delete.

### Changing authorization types

1. Go to the group page.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Group Setting` button.
1. Click the `Group Member` tab.
1. Click the combobox to the right of a member and select an authorization type.

Authorizations
---------------

`Group Admin` has the same authorization as a project admin on all of the group's projects, can
see all of the group's projects on the group page, and can configure group settings.

`Group Participant` has the same authorization as a project participant on the group's protected
and public projects, and can see those projects on the group page.

For more information, see [technical/access-control.md](../../guide/technical/access-control.md)
