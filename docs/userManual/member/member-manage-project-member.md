# Managing project members

You can manage project members if you're a project manager. You can delete members and change
their authorization.

There are two kinds of authorization, member and manager. Please refer to
[technical/access-control.md](../../guide/technical/access-control.md) (Korean only —
legacy never had an English version of this doc, only `docs/ko/technical/access-control.md`).

Changing member authorization
-------------------------------

1. Go to a project.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Project Settings` button.
1. Click the `Member` tab.
1. Click the dropdown box to the right of the member.

Deleting members from a project
---------------------------------

1. Go to a project.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Project Settings` button.
1. Click the `Member` tab.
1. Click the 'Delete' button next to the member you want to delete.
1. Click the 'Yes' button on the popup.
