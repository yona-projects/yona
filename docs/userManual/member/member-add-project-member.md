# Member registration

You can collaborate with members once you're registered as a project member.

Requesting registration
-------------------------

1. Go to the project you want to join.
1. Click the upper-body-shaped button at the top right of the project page, to the left of the
   `Watch` button.
1. Click the `Send sign-up request` button on the popup.

The icon will turn blue, and you'll become a member once administrators accept your request.

Accepting requests
--------------------

If someone requests membership, you can see and accept it.

1. Go to the project.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Project Settings` button.
1. Click the `Member` tab.
1. Click the `Add` button next to the member.

Canceling requests
--------------------

1. Go to the project.
1. Click the upper-body-shaped button at the top right of the project page, to the left of the
   `Watch` button.
1. Click the `Cancel sign-up request` button on the popup.
