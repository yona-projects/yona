# Watching Projects

You can use `Watch` to receive notifications when there are changes in projects.

To watch the project:

1. Go to the project you want to watch.
1. Click the `Watch` button.
1. Click the `Watch` button on popup.

If you no longer need to watch the project, click the `Watching` button and the `Unwatch` button
on popup.

Notifications
-------------

Notifications will be displayed on the main page of yona and be sent to your email. You can see
notifications when some events occur. To get more information, see
[notification-setting.md](../notification/notification-setting.md).

### Setting notifications

You can turn notifications on or off in more detail.

1. Click your avatar on the top of a page.
1. Click `Account Settings`.
1. Click `Notification Settings` tab.
1. Turn on or off the notification you want to change.
