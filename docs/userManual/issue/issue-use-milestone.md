# Using milestones

`Milestone` is for issue management. After creating milestones and setting a due date, you can
check the progress of issues within a certain milestone and manage schedules effectively.

Creating new milestones
-----------------------

1. Go to a project.
1. Click the `Milestone` menu.
1. Click the `New milestone` button.
1. Fill out the form.
1. Click the `Save` button.

Assigning issues to milestones
-------------------------------

There are two ways to assign a milestone to an issue.

1. When creating an issue:
    1. Click the `Issue` menu.
    1. Click the `New issue` button.
    1. Click the `Milestone` dropbox to see the milestone list.
    1. Select a milestone.
    1. Click the `Save` button.
1. After creating an issue:
    1. Click the `Issue` menu.
    1. Click an issue you want to assign a milestone to.
    1. Click the `Milestone` dropdown box to see the milestone list.
    1. Select a milestone.

Closing milestones
--------------------

1. Click the `Milestone` menu.
1. Click the milestone you want to close.
1. Click the `Close milestone` button.

The milestone status will then show as `Closed`.
