# Using labels

`Label` is for tagging issues. Labels help you categorize and search issues.

A label has a name and a color, and belongs to a category.

One or more labels can be attached to an issue.

Category
--------

A label must belong to a `Category`, and a `Category` can have one or more labels.

A category has a name and a property, either `multiple labels` or `only single label`.

`multiple labels` means multiple labels in that category can be attached to an issue at once.

`only single label` means only one of the labels in that category can be attached to an issue.

Setting issue labels
---------------------

To create, delete, and edit issue labels, go to the `Issue Label` page. There are two ways:

1. Click the `Issue` menu.
1. Click the gear-shaped button at the bottom left of the page, to the right of the `Label`
   text.

or

1. Go to a project.
1. Click the gear-shaped `Project Settings` button on the right of the project page.
1. Click the `Issue Label` tab.

You must be a project manager to reach the `Issue Label` page.

Creating labels
----------------

1. Go to the `Issue Label` page.
1. Fill out the form.
1. Click the `Add label` button.
1. In the popup, click either `multiple labels` or `only single label`.

Editing category
-----------------

1. Go to the `Issue Label` page.
1. Click the `Edit category` button.
1. Edit the name or the category property.
1. Click the `Save` button.

Editing labels
---------------

1. Go to the `Issue Label` page.
1. Click the `Edit` button.
1. Edit the name, color, or category.
1. Click the `Save` button.

Deleting labels
----------------

1. Go to the `Issue Label` page.
1. Click the `Delete` button.
1. In the dialog that opens, click the `Confirm` button.

Attaching labels
-----------------

There are two ways to attach labels to issues.

### Attaching a label to one issue

1. Click the `Issue` menu.
1. Click an issue item.
1. Click the label box on the right of the issue page.
1. Choose a label.

### Attaching a label to multiple issues

1. Click the `Issue` menu.
1. Click the checkboxes to the left of the issues.
1. Click the `Attach label` dropdown box.
1. Select a label.
1. The label is attached to the selected issues.

Detaching labels
------------------

There are two ways to remove labels from issues.

### Detaching a label from one issue

1. Click the `Issue` menu.
1. Click an issue item.
1. Click the `X` next to the label in the label box.

### Detaching a label from multiple issues

1. Click the `Issue` menu.
1. Click the checkboxes to the left of the issues.
1. Click the `Detach label` dropdown box.
1. Select a label.
1. The label is detached from the selected issues.
