Yona {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed as {{ include "yona.fullname" . }} (one replica).

First start: open the site and create the administrator account on the welcome screen,
then restart the pod:  kubectl rollout restart statefulset/{{ include "yona.fullname" . }}
Back up the data volume (it holds config/secret.properties, ssh/ host keys, repositories and attachments) and the database.
The data volume is kept when you run helm uninstall; delete it yourself when you really want to.
